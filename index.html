Coherent Thread

Early warning of collapse, and collapse prevention for control systems, with proofs where the mathematics allows.

Abstract

A collapse in a high-consequence system is usually preceded by a warning, but the warning appears in sensors that also drift, freeze and respond to ordinary disturbances. Coherent Thread addresses both parts of the problem. The first part detects a collapse in progress from camera and telemetry streams, distinguishes a hazardous trend from a sensor fault or an ordinary disturbance, and trips before the failure. The second part keeps a controller away from collapse through stability certificates that refuse an unstable system, command limits that hold by construction, and optimal manoeuvres derived from their necessary conditions. Each result states whether it is proved or only tested in simulation.

Two flagships

Detect · Demonstrated

Collapse foresight

A monitor for camera feeds and plant telemetry. It trips before a collapsing or runaway process fails, and it does not alarm on scene cuts, camera faults or ordinary motion.

  • 96% of hazards detected on held-out clips
  • 0% false alarms on scene cuts; EWMA and CUSUM: 100%
  • 100% of camera faults identified as camera faults; conventional monitors: 0%

Measured on synthetic video. Estimating the time remaining before a collapse is under research. The monitor is not released as source code.

Results and method of evaluation

Prevent · Available

Collapse prevention with proofs

Control functions that keep a system inside its safe region and state what they guarantee.

  • Certify. Lyapunov certificates; an unstable system is refused, not certified.
  • Bound. Command limits and barrier controls that cannot exceed their limits, by construction.
  • Plan. Optimal manoeuvres, including on SO(3), from Pontryagin's conditions; non-convergence raises an error.

Proofs and simulation evidence are labelled separately. The source code and 10 theory notes are published on this site.

Principles

  • Proof and evidence are labelled separately. check_vdot_negative can only falsify a stability claim, never prove one. The smoothed quadrotor controller states that its stability is checked in simulation, not proved.
  • Failures are reported. lyapunov_equation refuses an unstable system instead of returning a false certificate. PMPShooting.solve raises an error when it does not converge.
  • Bounds hold by construction. tanh_sat and barrier_control cannot exceed their limits. smooth_always may raise a false alarm but cannot report a violated margin as safe.
  • Advice cannot override measurement. In the monitor, the vision-language model can raise the alert level to ALARM at most. It cannot lower a level or clear a trip.
  • Open questions are documented. Each of the 10 theory notes states its assumptions and what remains unresolved.

Purpose

Some systems cannot be restarted after they fail: an aircraft in flight, a process plant, a cooling or power loop. Most tooling for such systems is designed for speed, throughput or cost. Coherent Thread is designed to make the unrecoverable event less likely, and to state clearly where it cannot.

Limitations

This is research code. Its tests are simulations and known-answer checks. It has no safety certification and no independent audit, and it has not been validated on real plants or real footage. It is intended as one layer among several: a second opinion, a monitor or a means of testing an argument. It must not be the only barrier between a fault and a catastrophe.
